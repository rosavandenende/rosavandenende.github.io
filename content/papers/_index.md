My research connects **economic theory** to questions in **climate policy**. In my PhD, I studied how responsibility for greenhouse gas emissions should be allocated when production is organised in global supply chains, using tools from **network theory, game theory and axiomatic analysis**.

I currently use **decision theory** to design early warning systems for tipping risks.
